About

Understanding air pollution dynamics through aerosol research

I am a PhD researcher in Environmental Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar, working at the convergence of aerosol science, optical instrumentation, physics-informed machine learning, and environmental sensing.

My flagship doctoral research focuses on developing a patent-pending multi-wavelength, multi-angle optical scattering platform (MWMA) coupled with Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and aerosol dynamics modeling. Grounded in aerosol optical theories (Mie and Rayleigh scattering), this hardware-AI platform achieves real-time retrieval of particle size distributions, complex refractive index (n, k), single scattering albedo, and optical extinction down to ~10 nm at a fraction of laboratory instrument cost.

In parallel, my research encompasses large-scale Environmental Quality Monitoring Systems (EQMS), deploying dense low-cost IoT sensor networks alongside spatio-temporal deep learning models (CNNs, GNNs, LSTMs) for urban air quality forecasting, automated sensor calibration, and high-resolution pollution mapping.

My work aims to transform complex atmospheric and optical data into actionable insights that support scientific discovery, public health, and evidence-based environmental policy.

Research domains

Core focus areas

  1. 01

    Aerosol science & optical instrumentation

    Multi-wavelength, multi-angle laser scattering, particle size distribution (PNSD) reconstruction from optical measurements, complex refractive index (n, k), single scattering albedo, and aerosol dynamic evolution down to ~10 nm.

  2. 02

    Air quality monitoring & IoT sensor networks

    Real-time ambient and indoor air pollution assessment using dense low-cost IoT sensor networks (EQMS) for multi-pollutant monitoring (PM2.5, PM10, CO2, CO, TVOCs), automated calibration, and health risk analysis.

  3. 03

    Physics-informed AI & spatio-temporal modeling

    Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for optical scattering inversion and aerosol digital twins, alongside spatio-temporal deep learning (CNN, GNN, LSTM) for urban air quality forecasting and anomaly detection.
