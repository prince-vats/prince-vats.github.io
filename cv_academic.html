  • Indoor Air Monitoring: Quantified real-time indoor concentrations of PM10, PM2.5, PM1, and Black Carbon (BC) across university classrooms, hostels, and offices; observed severe exposures with PM2.5 (59.60 µg/m³) and PM10 (71.12 µg/m³) consistently exceeding Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) permissible standards.

  • Clinical Health Assessment: Administered spirometry pulmonary function tests on 303 student participants; detected 35.97% restrictive lung impairments, 34.65% obstructive patterns, and 74.59% asthma prevalence, demonstrating statistically significant correlations between indoor particulate exposure and pulmonary function degradation.
