Flagship project

Multi-Wavelength, Multi-Angle Optical Scattering Platform for Aerosol Size-Distribution Retrieval

Physics-Informed Neural Networks + custom optical hardware for aerosol size distribution & optical characterisation

Lead inventors: Prince Vats & Dr. Sameer Patel · Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar

Problem
“Air quality monitors tell you how dirty the air is. We tell you exactly what is in it in real time.”

Conventional air quality monitoring faces a critical tradeoff: low-cost sensors only estimate bulk particulate mass and cannot resolve particles <300 nm, while laboratory instruments (SMPS, APS) cost ₹50 Lakh–₹1.5 Crore+, require 5–10 minutes per scan, and rely on hazardous consumables (butanol, radioactive neutralizers).

Approach

This project introduces a patent-pending, mass-deployable optical scattering diagnostic platform combining multi-wavelength laser probing, multi-angle light collection, and physics-informed deep learning for real-time nanoparticle detection down to ~10 nm at a fraction of laboratory instrument cost.

Optical hardware & probing

  • Multi-λ & multi-θ optics: multi-wavelength laser probing + multi-angle photodetectors (9 unique optical signals)
  • Nanoparticle resolution: resolves particles from ~10 nm to coarse sizes
  • Real-time diagnostic: 1-second continuous response with no scan delays
  • Zero consumables: solid-state optics; no toxic chemicals or radioactive neutralizers
  • On-device edge AI: microprocessor inference with zero cloud dependency

Physics-based optical inversion

  • Aerosol optical theories: rigorous inversion of Mie & Rayleigh scattering physics via PINNs
  • Optical characterisation: inversion of complex refractive index (n, k), single scattering albedo, and extinction coefficient
  • Microphysical retrieval: continuous particle count (N), geometric mean diameter (μ), and geometric standard deviation (σg)
  • Robust generalization: validated across urban, industrial, rural, and seasonal regimes

Urban sensing & spatio-temporal AI

Environmental Quality Monitoring System (EQMS)

Urban air quality monitoring & spatio-temporal forecasting using low-cost IoT sensor networks

Approach

Developing a comprehensive environmental monitoring network integrating distributed low-cost IoT sensor nodes for continuous, high-density air quality surveillance across urban micro-environments. This project combines embedded hardware integration, automated field calibration against reference-grade regulatory monitors, and spatio-temporal deep learning architectures for pollution forecasting.

Key measurements

  • PM₁, PM₂.₅, PM₁₀ (particulate matter)
  • CO₂, CO (gaseous pollutants)
  • TVOCs (volatile organic compounds)
  • Meteorology: temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed
  • Environmental acoustics: ambient noise sensor

Technologies & methods

  • Dense IoT sensor networks (urban deployment)
  • Automated machine learning field calibration
  • CNN-BiLSTM for anomaly detection
  • Spatio-temporal deep learning (GNN, LSTM/GRU)
Outcomes
  • Dense sensor network deployed across the IIT Gandhinagar campus, with planned large-scale urban expansion (50–60 nodes) for real-time monitoring
  • Automated QA/QC and machine learning calibration pipelines mitigating environmental cross-sensitivities against regulatory reference monitors
  • 24-hour, 7-day, and multi-scale pollution forecasting enabling actionable air quality intelligence and public health intervention

Research domains

Research focus areas

01

Optical aerosol instrumentation

Multi-wavelength & multi-angle light scattering, physics-informed neural inversion, and real-time nanoparticle size distribution retrieval down to ~10 nm.

02

IoT sensor networks

Design, deployment, and real-time data integration of affordable sensor nodes. Focus on calibration accuracy and long-term sensor stability.

03

AI/ML calibration & inversion

Physics-informed neural networks for optical inverse problems and ML pipelines for field calibration of low-cost sensors against reference monitors.

04

Spatio-temporal modelling

Graph neural networks and transformer architectures for high-resolution pollutant forecasting. Meteorological feature integration and dynamics modeling.

05

Health risk assessment

Quantifying exposure-response relationships and health impacts from air pollution. Stochastic and deterministic risk modeling in industrial and ambient environments.

06

Aerosol dynamics & air twins

Indoor aerosol digital twins, real-time tracking of particle coagulation, surface deposition, ventilation rates, and time-reversal contamination source tracking.
