Teaching Assistant IIT Gandhinagar · Postgraduate & Advanced Undergrad

Fundamentals of Aerosol Science

Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering · Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar

Served as a Teaching Assistant for this core advanced course, guiding postgraduate (M.Tech, Ph.D.) and senior undergraduate students through the physical and chemical principles governing aerosol particles in ambient atmospheres and engineered systems. Assisted the course instructor in structuring assignments, delivering numerical problem-solving sessions, and mentoring students in analyzing complex experimental aerosol datasets.

🔬 Core Aerosol Mechanics

  • Particle size distributions (number, surface area, and mass metrics) and log-normal representation.
  • Stokes flow, Cunningham slip correction factor, terminal settling velocity, and aerodynamic equivalent diameter.
  • Brownian motion, particle diffusion coefficients, coagulation dynamics, and condensation-evaporation equilibria.

📡 Measurement & Instrumentation

  • Operational principles of Optical Particle Counters (OPC) and Condensation Particle Counters (CPC).
  • Differential Mobility Analyzers (DMA) and Scanning Mobility Particle Sizers (SMPS).
  • Inertial separation via impactors, cyclone inlets, and gravimetric filter-based sampling protocols.

💻 Data Analysis & Modeling

  • Guided students through Python-based data pipelines for inversion and cleaning of high-resolution size distribution spectra.
  • Atmospheric dispersion calculations, particulate optical depth (AOD), and light scattering/absorption regimes.
  • Human respiratory deposition models (nasopharyngeal, tracheobronchial, and alveolar dosimetry).

📝 Pedagogy & Mentorship

  • Conducted interactive problem-solving and doubt-clearing tutorial sessions for analytical derivations.
  • Designed challenging problem sets, real-world case study projects, and grading rubrics.
  • Evaluated term project submissions and provided individual academic support for research methodologies.

Graduate Teaching Fellow IIT Gandhinagar · First-Year B.Tech Cohort

ES 101: Engineering Graphics

Foundation Engineering Curriculum · Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar

Served as a Graduate Teaching Fellow (GTF) for the foundational first-year engineering graphics course, instructing a large cohort of over 100 undergraduate students across various engineering disciplines. Responsible for leading weekly studio and computer laboratory sessions that bridged classical manual drafting on drawing boards with cutting-edge 3D parametric computer-aided design (CAD).

📐 Manual Technical Drafting

  • Mastery of drafting instruments, line conventions, title blocks, and standard dimensioning rules (BIS / ISO).
  • First-angle and third-angle orthographic projections, isometric projections, and oblique technical views.
  • Sectioning of solid geometries (prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones) and determination of true shapes of sections.
  • Interpenetration of solids, development of lateral surfaces for fabrication, and auxiliary view projections.

💻 3D Parametric CAD Modeling

  • Autodesk Inventor: Parametric sketching, geometric and dimensional constraints, feature creation (extrusions, revolves, sweeps, lofts, shells, patterns).
  • Autodesk Fusion 360: Cloud-integrated 3D modeling, modern multi-body component workflows, and collaborative design pipelines.
  • Generation of production-ready 2D working drawings directly from 3D models with associative dimensions and callouts.

⚙️ Assemblies & Design Standards

  • Creating complex multi-part mechanical assemblies with kinematic joints, contact sets, and degree-of-freedom constraints.
  • Exploded assembly views, bill of materials (BOM), and assembly drawing generation.
  • Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) principles, fits and tolerances, and engineering interference checks.

🛠️ Studio Leadership & Mentorship

  • Conducted live step-by-step drafting demonstrations on the board and live interactive CAD walkthroughs.
  • Supervised hands-on end-of-semester group design projects involving reverse engineering and mechanical modeling.
  • Evaluated weekly manual drawing sheets and digital CAD assignments with meticulous, constructive technical feedback.
  • Conducted practical laboratory assessments and CAD viva examinations.
